# What is DustSwap?

DustSwap is a Base-network (chain ID 8453) app that combines a token swap experience with a rewards layer: Particle Points (PP), daily check-ins with streaks, quests, referrals, and leaderboards.

## What you can do on DustSwap

- **Swap** tokens on Base, powered in partnership with **OpenOcean** aggregated routing. See [Swaps](../swap-and-bridge/swaps.md).
- **DustSweep** — clean up small "dust" token balances into ETH, USDC, WETH, or USDT in one transaction. See [What is DustSweep?](../dustsweep/what-is-dustsweep.md).
- **Earn Particle Points (PP)** for swapping, sweeping, checking in daily, completing quests, and referring friends. See [PP Rewards](../rewards/pp-rewards.md).
- **Build a daily streak** that boosts your PP earnings up to 4×. See [Daily Check-In](../rewards/daily-check-in.md).
- **Complete quests** for one-time PP rewards. See [Quests Overview](../quests/quests-overview.md).
- **Refer friends** for a signup bonus and lifetime commission. See [How Referrals Work](../referrals/how-referrals-work.md).
- **Compare your standing** on the Particle Points, Referral, and Volume leaderboards. See [Leaderboard Overview](../leaderboards/leaderboard-overview.md).

## Network

DustSweep runs on **Base mainnet** (chain ID 8453). [Swap & Bridge](../swap-and-bridge/overview.md) works across Base and other major chains, including moving tokens between them.

> **User Safety Note**
> DustSwap only operates through `app.dustswap.wtf`. Particle Points have no monetary value today and cannot be bought or sold — treat any site or DM claiming otherwise as a scam. Official support is the DustSwap Discord.
