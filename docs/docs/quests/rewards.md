# Quest Rewards

## How quest rewards work

- Every quest pays a **fixed, one-time Particle Points (PP) reward**, shown on the quest card before you start.
- Quest rewards are paid once per quest, per account — they don't repeat.
- The [streak boost](../rewards/streak-boost-explained.md) does **not** apply to quest rewards; you get the listed amount regardless of your streak.
- Quest PP is added to your total PP and counts toward the [PP leaderboard](../leaderboards/leaderboard-overview.md).

## Where to see reward amounts

Reward amounts are shown per-quest on the in-app [Quest board](quests-overview.md) and are admin-managed, so the list and amounts can change — the in-app board is the source of truth.
