# Troubleshooting

## Sweep / swap issues

See the dedicated [DustSweep FAQ](../dustsweep/faq.md) for sweep-specific issues (partial sweeps, failed transactions, refunds).

## "My transaction succeeded but I got no PP"

Points are credited after server-side verification of the transaction. Wait a moment and refresh. If it persists, the action may have hit its daily cap, or the transaction wasn't made through the app flow. See [PP FAQ](../rewards/pp-faq.md).

## "My streak broke"

If your streak recently broke, you may be able to restore it for a $1 fee — see [Streak Recovery](../rewards/streak-recovery.md).

## "My referral isn't showing"

The referral leaderboard refreshes on a periodic snapshot (every few hours); your Profile stats update sooner. See [Referral FAQ](../referrals/referral-faq.md).

## "I can't connect my X or Discord account"

See [Linking X & Discord](../quests/social-quests.md) — each social account can only be linked to one DustSwap account at a time.

## Still stuck?

Ask in the official DustSwap Discord — see [Official Links](../introduction/official-links.md).
