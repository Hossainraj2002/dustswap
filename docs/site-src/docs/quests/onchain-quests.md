# On-Chain Quests

On-chain quests are one-time tasks verified against your real DustSwap activity on Base — no social account linking required.

## How they're verified

When you take a qualifying action in the app (for example, sweeping dust via [DustSweep](../dustsweep/what-is-dustsweep.md)), DustSwap's backend checks your verified transaction history and automatically marks matching quests as complete.

## Example on-chain quests

| Quest | Trigger |
|---|---|
| First sweep | Complete your first DustSweep sweep |
| Sweep 10+ tokens | Sweep at least 10 different tokens (cumulative) |
| Sweep $100+ | Reach $100 of cumulative sweep value |
| Complete 5 sweeps | Complete 5 separate sweep transactions |

The full, current quest list is shown on the in-app [Quest board](../quests/quests-overview.md) — quests are database-driven and may be added or changed by DustSwap.

## Rewards

Each on-chain quest pays a **fixed, one-time PP reward** shown on the quest card. Quest rewards do **not** receive the streak boost. See [How Quests Work](quests-overview.md) for the general reward rules.
