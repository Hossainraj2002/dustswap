# \$DUST Tokenomics

\$DUST is the native token of Dustswap, issued on Base with a fixed supply of 100,000,000. This page sets out the complete supply breakdown: what each allocation funds, how much unlocks at TGE, and the vesting schedule that governs every remaining token.

## Token overview

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| **Ticker** | \$DUST |
| **Network** | Base |
| **Total supply** | 100,000,000 (fixed) |
| **Unlocked at TGE** | 18,000,000 (18% of total supply) |
| **Locked at TGE** | 82,000,000 (82% of total supply) |
| **Liquidity routing** | OpenOcean routed liquidity |

## Allocation

The full supply is divided across five allocations. No portion of the supply sits outside this table.

| Allocation | Share | Tokens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ecosystem | 30% | 30,000,000 |
| Liquidity & CEX | 20% | 20,000,000 |
| Community & Airdrop | 20% | 20,000,000 |
| Team & Investor | 20% | 20,000,000 |
| Marketing | 10% | 10,000,000 |
| **Total** | **100%** | **100,000,000** |

## What each allocation funds

**Ecosystem (30%)** Reserved for strategic partnerships, developer grants, and building core products that expand protocol utility.

**Liquidity & CEX (20%)** Provides depth to \$DUST markets and supports market stability across exchange venues.

**Community & Airdrop (20%)** Distributed to early adopters and active users, so that ownership is broadly held rather than concentrated at launch.

**Team & Investor (20%)** Long-term allocation for the core team and backers, subject to a cliff and linear vesting so that incentives track the protocol's progress rather than its launch.

**Marketing (10%)** Dedicated budget for awareness and user acquisition, including creators, community campaigns, and partnerships.

## Release profile after TGE

The following rates follow directly from the schedule above.

- **Ecosystem** releases 30,000,000 evenly across 36 months, approximately 833,333 \$DUST per month, starting at TGE.
- **Liquidity & CEX** releases its remaining 10,000,000 evenly across 24 months, approximately 416,667 \$DUST per month.
- **Team & Investor** releases its remaining 18,000,000 evenly across 12 months after a 2 month cliff, 1,500,000 \$DUST per month, fully vested 14 months after TGE.
- **Marketing** releases its remaining 8,000,000 in annual tranches of one third.
- **Community & Airdrop** releases its remaining 16,000,000 on a partner dependent schedule, tied to distribution programs rather than a fixed calendar.

Ecosystem is the last allocation to finish vesting, at month 36.
